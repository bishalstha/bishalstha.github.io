Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by our app and how it is used.

Information Collection and Use

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require you to provide any personal information to use the app.

Use of Phone Features

Our application is built with a strict zero-collection policy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. To provide its core functionality, the app utilizes several advanced Android permissions:

  • Accessibility Service: Used solely to monitor foreground apps; no screen content or keystrokes are recorded or uploaded.
  • Overlay (Display Over Other Apps): Used only to display lock screen to protect locked apps.
  • Notification: Used to display notifications for the app's actions.
  • Device Administrator (Optional, User-Initiated): Utilized exclusively for disabling unauthorized users from resetting or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Our app does not use any third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.
